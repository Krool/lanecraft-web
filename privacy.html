Privacy Policy

Lanecraft · Effective March 15, 2026 · Updated July 30, 2026

Lanecraft ("the Game") is a free game made by Krool ("we", "us"). This policy covers both the Game and this website. There is no advertising, no in-app purchasing, and no account to create.

What we collect

The Game uses Firebase (Google) for optional online multiplayer. If you play online, this data is involved:

  • Anonymous identifier. Firebase Authentication issues a random anonymous ID for your device. No email, name, or login is required.
  • Match sync data. Game commands and synchronization state are stored in Firebase Realtime Database for the length of the match, then deleted.

If you never play online, none of the above is created.

Analytics

This website and the Game use Google Analytics to count visits and see which features get used. It records anonymous usage events, never personal information.

In the Game, analytics is off until you agree to it. You are asked on the main menu, and nothing is sent before you answer. On this website, analytics loads with the page.

What we do not collect

  • No real name, email address, or phone number.
  • No location data.
  • No advertising SDKs and no ad tracking. The Game contains no advertising.
  • We do not sell your data or share it with anyone beyond the Google services described here.

Stored on your device

Your progress, settings, player profile, achievements, and unlocks are saved locally through browser local storage. That data stays on your device and is never uploaded.

Third-party services

Firebase and Google Analytics are both operated by Google, and Google's privacy policy governs their handling: policies.google.com/privacy

Children

The Game does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Only anonymous identifiers and locally stored display names of your own choosing are used, so no personal information is collected from any player.

Deleting your data

Clear the Game's local storage, or uninstall it, and the on-device data is gone. Multiplayer sync data is deleted from Firebase automatically once a match ends. To withdraw analytics consent in the Game, turn analytics off in settings.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated date at the top will change with it.

Contact

Questions go to github.com/Krool/lanecraft-web/issues
